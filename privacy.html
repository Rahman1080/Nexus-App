Application: Toolsy (formerly Nexus | Package ID: com.ar.nexusapp)

This Privacy Policy describes how Toolsy ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, handles, and protects user data across our mobile application and services.

1. Data Collection & Use

  • Account Credentials: If you choose to create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials securely via Firebase Authentication.
  • Photos & Videos (Media): When you use utilities like Image Editor or Image-to-PDF, photos are accessed solely through the system photo picker on your local device. We do not upload or store your personal photos or videos on our servers. All image processing occurs strictly on your device.
  • Files & Documents: User-created notes, tasks, and document metadata are stored in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Usage & Diagnostics: Feature usage counts (e.g. AI requests, PDF conversions) to manage plan limits.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

  • Google AdMob: Serves banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and advertising IDs. Google Advertising Privacy
  • Firebase Services: Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and App Check security. Google Privacy Policy
  • Stripe: Securely processes in-app subscriptions and payments. Toolsy never sees or stores full credit card numbers. Stripe Privacy Policy
