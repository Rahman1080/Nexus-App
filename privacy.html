This Privacy Policy explains how Toolsy ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and enhance our suite of productivity and utility tools:

  • Account Credentials: If you register or log in, we collect your email address and authentication identifiers via Firebase Authentication.
  • User-Generated Content: Notes, tasks, and calculator preferences that you save within the app, stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Usage & Diagnostic Data: Feature usage counts (e.g. AI requests, PDF conversions) to manage tier limits and application stability.
  • Device Identifiers: Advertising identifiers, device model, and OS version collected by third-party SDKs to serve contextual ads and analytics.

2. Third-Party Services

The app integrates trusted third-party providers that handle data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: Used for authentication, Firestore database, and crash analytics. Google Privacy Policy
