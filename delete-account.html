Account & Data Deletion Policy

At Toolsy, we are committed to full data transparency and give users complete ownership over their account, personal information, and data. This policy details how you can request deletion of your account and all associated data, including notes, tasks, files, and temporary media.

1. How to Request Account & Data Deletion

We provide two simple methods for users to request permanent deletion of their account and data:

Option A: In-App Immediate Deletion (Recommended)

If you currently have the Toolsy application installed:

  1. Open the Toolsy app.
  2. Tap the Profile icon in the bottom/side navigation menu.
  3. Scroll down to the bottom of the screen.
  4. Tap the red Delete Account button.
  5. Confirm by tapping Confirm Delete.

✓ Result: Your account, authentication credentials, profile data, notes, and tasks are deleted immediately in real time.

Option B: Web & Email Deletion Request (Without App Installed)

If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to submit an external deletion request:

  1. Send an email to our support team at: nexusapp49@gmail.com
  2. Subject Line: Account & Data Deletion Request - Toolsy
  3. Include the email address registered with your Toolsy account.

Submit Deletion Request via Email

2. Data Types Deleted & Handled

A. Photos and Videos (Media)

Toolsy features utilities like Image Editor and Image-to-PDF converter.

  • Local On-Device Processing: Photos and videos selected by the user are processed strictly on the local device within transient application memory.
  • No Remote Photo/Video Storage: We do not upload, harvest, or store personal photos or videos to any remote servers.
  • Deletion: All temporary image cache generated during PDF creation or image editing is deleted automatically when the task finishes, upon app cache clear, or upon account deletion.

B. Files, Documents & Notes

  • All saved user notes, checklists, task items, and calculator records stored in Google Cloud Firestore are permanently wiped upon deletion.
  • Generated PDF output files stored in application-specific storage are deleted.

C. User Authentication & Profile Data

  • Email address, User UID, and authentication tokens in Firebase Authentication are permanently deleted.
  • User preference settings and AI usage quota logs are completely erased.

3. Data Retention Policy & Exceptions

Legal & Financial Compliance Retention

In compliance with Google Play policies and governing tax/financial legislation:

  • Billing Transactions: If you purchased a premium subscription via Stripe, transaction records (e.g. invoice IDs, dates, and amounts) must be maintained to fulfill legal bookkeeping, taxation reporting, and anti-fraud mandates.
  • Security: Toolsy never stores raw credit card or bank details. Payment records are securely handled by Stripe.
  • Retention Timeline: Such records are stored only for the statutory duration required by law and are thereafter securely purged.

4. Deletion Processing Timeline

  • In-App Deletion: Executed instantly.
  • Email / Web Requests: Verified and completed within 48 to 72 business hours (and no later than 30 days). A confirmation email will be sent once the deletion is finalized.
