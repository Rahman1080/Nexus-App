At Toolsy, we respect your privacy and provide transparent, straightforward options for users to delete their account and associated data at any time in compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can request full deletion of your account and data through either of the following two methods:

Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

If you currently have the Toolsy app installed on your device:

  1. Open the Toolsy app.
  2. Tap the Profile icon in the navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm your choice.

Result: Your authentication credentials and all user profile records are deleted immediately.

Option 2: External Web / Email Request (Without App Installed)

If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to request deletion via the web:

  1. Send an email to our official support team at: nexusapp49@gmail.com
  2. Use the Subject line: Account and Data Deletion Request - Toolsy
  3. Include the registered email address associated with your Toolsy account.

Types of Data That Are Deleted

Upon receiving and confirming an account deletion request, the following data is permanently purged:

  • User Authentication Credentials: Email address, User UID, login metadata, and linked auth identifiers stored in Firebase Authentication.
  • Personal Profile Data: Display name, profile preferences, and account configuration stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • User Created Content: All personal notes, checklist items, and task entries.
  • Usage & Analytics Logs: AI usage quotas, history logs, and download counters associated with your UID.

Data That May Be Retained & Legal Justification

Retention Policy

In accordance with Google Play developer policies and applicable law:

  • Financial & Billing Records: If you made an in-app purchase or active subscription processed via Stripe, transactional payment records (such as invoice ID, timestamp, and amount) are retained as required by financial, taxation, and anti-fraud regulations. We do not store full credit card numbers.
  • Retention Period: Any legally required financial records are retained only for the minimum duration mandated by local tax law, after which they are securely purged.

Deletion Timeline

- In-App Deletion: Instantaneous upon user confirmation.
- Web / Email Requests: Verified and executed within 48 to 72 business hours (and no later than 30 days). A confirmation email is dispatched once deletion is concluded.
